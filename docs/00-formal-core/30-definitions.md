# ASM Definitions v1.0

## Purpose

This document defines the fundamental concepts of the Formal Core of the
Accounting State Model (ASM).

The definitions specify the mathematical objects used in ASM.

Domain-specific concepts are introduced in higher layers.

---

## Reality

**Definition:**

Reality is the domain containing objects and changes that exist
independently of the ASM model.

**Symbol:**

$$
\mathcal{R}
$$

**Meaning:**

Reality is the source from which Evidence is obtained.

---

## Evidence

**Definition:**

Evidence is observable information obtained from Reality.

**Symbol:**

$$
\mathcal{Z}
$$

**Formal relationship:**

$$
Obs\subseteq\mathcal{R}\times\mathcal{Z}
$$

**Meaning:**

Evidence is not Reality itself.

$$
\mathcal{Z}\neq\mathcal{R}
$$

Evidence is an observation of Reality.

---

## Event

**Definition:**

An Event is an occurrence represented in ASM that produces an Effect.

**Symbol:**

$$
e
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
e\in\mathcal{E}
$$

**Meaning:**

An Event represents the cause of a state change.

An Event itself is not a State change.

---

## Interpretation

**Definition:**

Interpretation is the process that transforms Evidence into Events
according to Policies and Parameters.

**Symbol:**

$$
I
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
I:
\mathcal{Z}
\times
\Theta
\times
\mathcal{M}
\rightarrow
\mathcal{E}
$$

**Meaning:**

Interpretation determines how observed information is represented as an
Event.

---

## Policy

**Definition:**

A Policy is a rule system used during Interpretation.

**Symbol:**

$$
\theta
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
\theta\in\Theta
$$

**Meaning:**

A Policy determines the rules applied when generating Events.

---

## Parameter

**Definition:**

A Parameter is a value or assumption used during Interpretation.

**Symbol:**

$$
m
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
m\in\mathcal{M}
$$

**Meaning:**

Parameters provide additional information required for Interpretation.

---

## History

**Definition:**

History is an ordered sequence of Events and their associated times.

**Symbol:**

$$
H_t
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
H_t\in(\mathcal{E}\times T)^*
$$

or:

$$
H_t=((e_1,t_1),(e_2,t_2),\dots,(e_n,t_n))
$$

where:

$$
e_i\in\mathcal{E}
$$

and:

$$
t_i\in T
$$

**Meaning:**

History records the sequence of Events accumulated up to time $t$.

---

## Effect

**Definition:**

An Effect is the change in State generated by an Event.

**Symbol:**

$$
\alpha(e)
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
\alpha:
\mathcal{E}
\rightarrow
\Delta\mathcal{S}
$$

and:

$$
\alpha(e)\in\Delta\mathcal{S}
$$

**Meaning:**

Effect describes what changes when an Event occurs.

The relationship is:

$$
Event
\rightarrow
Effect
$$

---

## State Space

**Definition:**

State Space is the set of all possible conditions of the modeled system.

**Symbol:**

$$
\mathcal{S}
$$

---

## State

**Definition:**

State is the condition of the modeled system at a specific time.

**Symbol:**

$$
S_t
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
S_t\in\mathcal{S}
$$

**Meaning:**

State represents the information required to describe the current system
condition.

---

## State Transition

**Definition:**

State Transition is the process of applying an Effect to a State and
obtaining a new State.

**Symbol:**

$$
\delta
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
\delta:
\mathcal{S}
\times
\Delta\mathcal{S}
\rightarrow
\mathcal{S}
$$

such that:

$$
S_{t+1}
=
\delta(S_t,\alpha(e))
$$

**Meaning:**

The Transition Function determines how an Effect changes the current
State.

---

## State Generation

**Definition:**

State Generation is the transformation from History into State.

**Symbol:**

$$
G
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
G:
\mathcal{H}
\rightarrow
\mathcal{S}
$$

such that:

$$
S_t=G(H_t)
$$

**Meaning:**

State is derived from accumulated History.

---

## Representation

**Definition:**

Representation is a transformation of History into information suitable
for a specific purpose.

**Symbol:**

$$
F_Q
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
F_Q:
\mathcal{H}
\rightarrow
\mathcal{Y}_Q
$$

where:

$$
Q\in\mathcal{Q}
$$

**Meaning:**

Different Tasks may require different representations of the same
History.

---

## Task

**Definition:**

Task is the purpose that determines required information.

**Symbol:**

$$
Q
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
Q\in\mathcal{Q}
$$

---

## Constraint

**Definition:**

A Constraint is a condition that defines valid States.

**Symbol:**

$$
\kappa
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
\kappa:
\mathcal{S}
\rightarrow
\mathbb{R}
$$

A valid State satisfies:

$$
\kappa(S_t)=0
$$

**Meaning:**

Constraints define properties that must be preserved.

---

## Valid Transition

**Definition:**

A Valid Transition is a State Transition that preserves required
Constraints.

**Formal expression:**

If:

$$
\kappa(S_t)=0
$$

and:

$$
Valid(e)
$$

then:

$$
\kappa(S_{t+1})=0
$$

where:

$$
S_{t+1}
=
\delta(S_t,\alpha(e))
$$

**Meaning:**

Valid Events preserve system consistency.

---

## Formal Core Structure

The fundamental structure of ASM is:

$$
Reality
\rightarrow
Evidence
\rightarrow
Event
\rightarrow
Effect
\rightarrow
State
\rightarrow
Representation
$$

The mathematical relationship is:

$$
z
\rightarrow
e
\rightarrow
\alpha(e)
\rightarrow
S_{t+1}
$$
