# ASM Axioms v2.0

## Purpose

This document defines the fundamental axioms of the Accounting State
Model (ASM).

The axioms specify the assumptions required to construct the ASM
framework.

An axiom is a foundational assumption and is not derived from other ASM
concepts.

---

## Observation Principle

**Statement:**

Accounting does not directly operate on Reality.

Accounting operates on Evidence obtained through observation.

**Formal expression:**

$$
Obs\subseteq\mathcal{R}\times\mathcal{Z}
$$

where:

- $\mathcal{R}$ is Reality Space.
- $\mathcal{Z}$ is Evidence Space.
- $Obs$ is the observation relation.

**Explanation:**

Reality and Evidence are different mathematical objects.

A single reality instance may correspond to multiple evidence instances.

Therefore:

$$
\exists z_1,z_2\in\mathcal{Z},
(r,z_1)\in Obs
\land
(r,z_2)\in Obs
$$

is possible.

**Consequence:**

Accounting represents observed information rather than Reality itself.

$$
Reality\neq Accounting
$$

---

## Interpretation Principle

**Statement:**

Accounting Events are generated by interpreting Evidence according to
Accounting Policy and Estimates.

**Formal expression:**

$$
\widehat{\mathsf{Acc}}_{\theta}:
\mathcal{Z}\times H_{\eta}
\rightarrow
\mathcal{E}_{acc}
$$

where:

- $\theta\in\Theta$ is Accounting Policy.
- $\eta\in H_{\eta}$ is an Estimate.
- $\mathcal{E}_{acc}$ is Accounting Event Space.

**Explanation:**

The same Evidence may produce different Accounting Events depending on
Accounting Policy.

For:

$$
\theta_1,\theta_2\in\Theta
$$

it is possible that:

$$
\theta_1\neq\theta_2
$$

and:

$$
\widehat{\mathsf{Acc}}_{\theta_1}(z,\eta)
\neq
\widehat{\mathsf{Acc}}_{\theta_2}(z,\eta)
$$

**Consequence:**

Accounting information is an interpreted representation of economic
reality.

## Historical Persistence Principle

**Statement:**

Accounting Events form an ordered and persistent history.

**Formal expression:**

$$
H_A(t)
=
((e_1,t_1),(e_2,t_2),\dots,(e_n,t_n))
$$

For:

$$
t_1<t_2
$$

the history satisfies:

$$
H_A(t_1)\subseteq H_A(t_2)
$$

**Explanation:**

New Accounting Events extend Accounting History.

Past events are not removed from history without explicit accounting
mechanisms.

**Consequence:**

Corrections and adjustments are represented as additional Accounting
Events.

---

## Representation Principle

**Statement:**

Accounting History can be transformed into Task-dependent
representations.

**Formal expression:**

$$
\forall Q\in\mathcal{Q},
\exists F_Q:
\mathcal{H}_{acc}
\rightarrow
Y_Q
$$

where:

- $\mathcal{Q}$ is Task Space.
- $F_Q$ is a Task-dependent representation function.
- $Y_Q$ is the representation space.

**Explanation:**

A single Accounting History can produce different representations.

For:

$$
Q_1,Q_2\in\mathcal{Q}
$$

where:

$$
Q_1\neq Q_2
$$

it is possible that:

$$
F_{Q_1}(H_A)
\neq
F_{Q_2}(H_A)
$$

**Consequence:**

Financial accounting, management accounting, and operational systems are
different projections of the same Accounting History.

## Effect Generation Principle

**Statement:**

Every Accounting Event generates an Accounting Effect.

**Formal expression:**

$$
\forall e\in\mathcal{E}_{acc},
\exists\alpha(e)\in\Delta\mathcal{X}
$$

such that:

$$
\alpha:
\mathcal{E}_{acc}
\rightarrow
\Delta\mathcal{X}
$$

**Explanation:**

Accounting Events do not directly change reports.

They first generate changes in accounting dimensions.

$$
e
\rightarrow
\alpha(e)
\rightarrow
Representation
$$

**Consequence:**

Accounting Effects are the fundamental bridge between Events and States.

---

## Valid Transition Preservation Principle

**Statement:**

A valid Accounting Event transition preserves required constraints.

**Formal expression:**

$$
\forall e\in\mathcal{E}_{acc},
\forall t\in T
$$

if:

$$
Valid(e)
\land
C(S_t)=0
$$

then:

$$
C(S_{t+1})=0
$$

where:

- $S_t$ is Operational State.
- $C$ is a constraint function.

**Explanation:**

Valid transitions preserve invariants of the accounting system.

Examples:

Double-entry constraint:

$$
\mathsf{Bal}_B(B)=0
$$

Financial position constraint:

$$
A_t-L_t-E_t=0
$$

**Consequence:**

Accounting consistency is maintained through valid state transitions.

## Axiom Summary

| Axiom | Principle | Formal Object |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observation Principle | Reality is observed through Evidence | $Obs$ |
| Interpretation Principle | Evidence becomes Accounting Events | $\widehat{\mathsf{Acc}}_\theta$ |
| Historical Persistence Principle | Events form persistent history | $H_A$ |
| Representation Principle | History generates Task representations | $F_Q$ |
| Effect Generation Principle | Events generate state changes | $\alpha(e)$ |
| Valid Transition Preservation Principle | Valid transitions preserve constraints | $C(S)$ |
