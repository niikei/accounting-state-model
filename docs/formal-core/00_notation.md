# ASM Notation v1.0

## Purpose

This document defines the official mathematical notation used in the
Formal Core of the Accounting State Model (ASM).

All Formal Core documents MUST follow the notation defined here.

The Formal Core defines an abstract state transition model.
Accounting-specific concepts are introduced in higher layers.

---

## General Mathematical Convention

A mathematical space is represented by a calligraphic letter.

Example:

$$
\mathcal{X}
$$

An element belonging to a space is represented by a lowercase letter.

Example:

$$
x\in\mathcal{X}
$$

A function is represented by a mapping.

Example:

$$
f:X\rightarrow Y
$$

A relation is represented as a subset of a Cartesian product.

Example:

$$
R\subseteq X\times Y
$$

## Time Notation

### Time Space

**Symbol:**

$$
T
$$

**Definition:**

The domain of temporal points used in ASM.

---

### Time Point

**Symbol:**

$$
t
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
t\in T
$$

---

### Period

**Symbol:**

$$
I
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
I=[t_0,t_1]\subseteq T
$$

**Definition:**

A temporal interval used to aggregate changes.

---

## Reality and Observation

### Reality Space

**Symbol:**

$$
\mathcal{R}
$$

**Definition:**

The space representing objects and changes that exist independently of
the ASM model.

---

### Evidence Space

**Symbol:**

$$
\mathcal{Z}
$$

**Definition:**

The space of observable information obtained from Reality.

---

### Observation Relation

**Symbol:**

$$
Obs
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
Obs\subseteq\mathcal{R}\times\mathcal{Z}
$$

**Definition:**

The relation connecting Reality and Evidence.

---

## Event Notation

### Event Space

**Symbol:**

$$
\mathcal{E}
$$

**Definition:**

The space of events represented in ASM.

---

### Event

**Symbol:**

$$
e
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
e\in\mathcal{E}
$$

**Definition:**

An occurrence that produces a change in the modeled system.

---

## Interpretation Notation

### Policy Space

**Symbol:**

$$
\Theta
$$

**Definition:**

The space of policies or rules used for interpretation.

---

### Policy

**Symbol:**

$$
\theta
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
\theta\in\Theta
$$

---

### Parameter Space

**Symbol:**

$$
\mathcal{M}
$$

**Definition:**

The space of parameters or assumptions used during interpretation.

---

### Parameter

**Symbol:**

$$
m
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
m\in\mathcal{M}
$$

---

### Interpretation Function

**Symbol:**

$$
I
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
I:
\mathcal{Z}
\times
\Theta
\times
\mathcal{M}
\rightarrow
\mathcal{E}
$$

**Definition:**

A function that transforms Evidence into Events according to Policy and
Parameters.

---

## History Notation

### History Space

**Symbol:**

$$
\mathcal{H}
$$

**Definition:**

The space of event histories.

---

### History

**Symbol:**

$$
H(t)
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
H(t)\in(\mathcal{E}\times T)^*
$$

**Definition:**

A finite ordered sequence of Events and their associated times.

---

### Event Sequence

**Formal expression:**

$$
H(t)=((e_1,t_1),(e_2,t_2),\dots,(e_n,t_n))
$$

where:

$$
e_i\in\mathcal{E}
$$

and:

$$
t_i\in T
$$

---

## State Notation

### State Space

**Symbol:**

$$
\mathcal{S}
$$

**Definition:**

The space of possible system states.

---

### State

**Symbol:**

$$
S_t
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
S_t\in\mathcal{S}
$$

**Definition:**

The condition of the modeled system at time $t$.

---

### State Generation Function

**Symbol:**

$$
G
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
G:
\mathcal{H}
\rightarrow
\mathcal{S}
$$

**Definition:**

A function generating a State from History.

---

### State Transition Function

**Symbol:**

$$
\delta
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
S_{t+1}
=
\delta(S_t,e)
$$

**Definition:**

A function describing state transition caused by an Event.

---

## Effect Notation

### Effect Space

**Symbol:**

$$
\Delta\mathcal{S}
$$

**Definition:**

The space of possible state changes.

---

### Effect Function

**Symbol:**

$$
\alpha
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
\alpha:
\mathcal{E}
\rightarrow
\Delta\mathcal{S}
$$

**Definition:**

A function mapping an Event to its state change.

---

### Effect

**Formal expression:**

$$
\alpha(e)=\Delta S
$$

**Definition:**

The state change generated by an Event.

---

## Representation Notation

### Task Space

**Symbol:**

$$
\mathcal{Q}
$$

**Definition:**

The space of purposes requiring information.

---

### Task

**Symbol:**

$$
Q
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
Q\in\mathcal{Q}
$$

---

### Representation Space

**Symbol:**

$$
\mathcal{Y}
$$

**Definition:**

The space of representations generated for Tasks.

---

### Task-dependent Representation

**Symbol:**

$$
F_Q
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
F_Q:
\mathcal{H}
\rightarrow
\mathcal{Y}_Q
$$

**Definition:**

A transformation from History into a representation suitable for Task
$Q$.

---

## Validity Notation

### Constraint Space

**Symbol:**

$$
\mathcal{C}
$$

**Definition:**

The space of constraints required for valid States.

---

### Constraint Function

**Symbol:**

$$
C
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
C:
\mathcal{S}
\rightarrow
\mathbb{R}
$$

---

### Valid State

**Formal expression:**

$$
C(S_t)=0
$$

**Definition:**

A State satisfying required constraints.

---

## ASM Core Flow

The Formal Core is represented as:

$$
\mathcal{R}
\rightarrow
\mathcal{Z}
\rightarrow
\mathcal{E}
\rightarrow
\Delta\mathcal{S}
\rightarrow
\mathcal{S}
$$

or:

$$
z
\rightarrow
e
\rightarrow
\alpha(e)
\rightarrow
S_{t+1}
$$
