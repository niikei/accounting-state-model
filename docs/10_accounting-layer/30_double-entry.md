# ASM Double Entry v1.0

## Purpose

This document defines Double Entry as a representation system for
Accounting Effects.

In ASM, Double Entry is not the source of accounting meaning.

Instead, Double Entry represents changes in Accounting State generated by
Accounting Events.

---

## Double Entry Representation

An Accounting Effect is:

$$
\alpha_{acc}(e_{acc})
\in
\Delta\mathcal{S}_{acc}
$$

A Double Entry representation function maps an Accounting Effect into a
journal representation.

**Symbol:**

$$
J
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
J:
\Delta\mathcal{S}_{acc}
\rightarrow
\mathcal{J}
$$

where:

- $\mathcal{J}$ represents the space of journal entries.

---

## Journal Entry

**Definition:**

A Journal Entry is a representation of an Accounting Effect using Debit and
Credit coordinates.

**Symbol:**

$$
j
$$

**Formal expression:**

$$
j\in\mathcal{J}
$$

---

## Double Entry Constraint

A valid Journal Entry must satisfy the balancing condition.

Let:

- $D(j)$ represent total Debit amount.
- $C(j)$ represent total Credit amount.

The Double Entry Constraint is:

$$
D(j)-C(j)=0
$$

Therefore:

$$
D(j)=C(j)
$$

---

## Effect Preservation

A valid Double Entry representation preserves the Accounting Effect.

Formally:

For:

$$
\alpha_{acc}(e_{acc})
\in
\Delta\mathcal{S}_{acc}
$$

there exists:

$$
j\in\mathcal{J}
$$

such that:

$$
J(\alpha_{acc}(e_{acc}))=j
$$

and:

$$
Effect(j)
=
\alpha_{acc}(e_{acc})
$$

---

## Example: Cash Sale

Consider the Accounting Event:

$$
e_{sale}\in\mathcal{E}_{acc}
$$

representing:

"Goods are sold for 100 units of cash."

The Accounting Effect is:

$$
\alpha_{acc}(e_{sale})
=
(+100Cash,+100Revenue)
$$

The Double Entry representation is:

| Account | Debit | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cash | 100 | |
| Revenue | | 100 |

The Journal Entry satisfies:

$$
D(j)=100
$$

and:

$$
C(j)=100
$$

therefore:

$$
D(j)-C(j)=0
$$

---

## Meaning of Debit and Credit

Debit and Credit are not independent economic concepts.

They are coordinates used to represent changes in Accounting State.

Therefore:

$$
Debit/Credit
\neq
Economic\ Reality
$$

and:

$$
Debit/Credit
=
Representation\ of\ Accounting\ Effect
$$

---

## Double Entry as Constraint Satisfaction

The purpose of Double Entry is to ensure that represented Effects preserve
Accounting Constraints.

A valid Journal Entry satisfies:

$$
D(j)-C(j)=0
$$

and the resulting Accounting State satisfies:

$$
\kappa_{acc}(S_{acc,t+1})=0
$$

Therefore:

$$
Journal
\rightarrow
Effect
\rightarrow
Valid\ State
$$

---

## Interpretation

Traditional accounting education often begins with:

$$
Debit=Left
$$

$$
Credit=Right
$$

ASM begins with:

$$
Accounting\ Effect
$$

and defines Debit/Credit as its representation.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
Double\ Entry
=
A\ balanced\ representation\ of\ Accounting\ State\ transitions
}
$$
